## Three bugs found on the way, all fixed

- **My own, first: the board took no fixes from 01:38 to 02:00.** The "keep zeroing a sensor that was not read" rule included the absent sensor 8, so the array's zero never finished and the locator waited on it. Now a zero under way for provisional sensors only (never in the fit until it is done) does not hold the fit, an absent sensor is not retried, and a sensor that turns up later takes its own provisional zero then.

- **A fake zero was being saved and restored.** The night's first boots had sensor 9 absent; its "zero" of 0,0,0 was written to the settings, and every boot since restored it as real, so the sensor never took a zero once it was wired (its field showed as the raw reading). Now a sensor without a zero gets no `zero=` line, a 0,0,0 line counts as none, and a sensor that was not read while the others zeroed keeps zeroing until it is. `m` shows y / uncal / nozero / prov in a "fit" column.
- **A wedged TMAG bus was no longer reset.** The V3F's rule was "every sensor failed two passes running"; with the MMC on bus 1 still answering, a wedged bus 0 never counted as "all", and the eight TMAGs failed for seconds until the slow recovery caught them (66 failed reads each, twice, in five minutes). The rule is per bus now.

Also: the bus check used to re-zero the array at its end (with whatever probe lay on the board) - it keeps the zero now; and a zero taken from live frames because the record had none (the probe's presence unknown) is "provisional": out of the fit until the locator settles it from a good fix of the others (the magnet's field at the sensor taken out of the zero if the reading is missing it, kept if the reading shows it), or `z` settles everything.

## For magcal, which is what the MMCs need next

`tools/magcal` has `only=<list>` (refine only those sensors against poses from the trusted ones, both faces tried, the rest held as the table has them) and `seen=<n>` (the probe's small magnet lights fewer sensors than the calibration magnet did; use 3), and it drops readings past 0.9 of a part's range (the MMC clips at 3 mT). Checked on synthetic recordings: an MMC at 137°, gain 1.05, face up or face down comes back as 137.1° / 137.2°, 1.05 / 1.03, the right face (the wrong face costs 25× more).
