# The magnet's strength learned, and a missing sensor's last reading carrying
# presence (2026-09-22, evening). `make check` runs this after check.txt.
# The regression runs with the MMC56x3 in (the board boots with it out: MAG_USE_MMC_AT_BOOT),
# and with the 2026-09-18 probe's magnet (4232) the scenes were written with, told to the
# locator as a known magnet (the firmware's default is the bench's current one).
:mmc on
k4232\r

@seed 7

# A far probe, seen plainly by the MMC56x3 alone: the MMC missing a frame now
# and then (its hand-wired bus does, about once a second on the bench) must
# not end the track - before, every missed frame ended it "nothing present"
# and the next frame found it again (1030 times in 24 minutes: a flicker).
@magnet 24 22 65 0 0 -1
@run 3000
l
@expect "z 6"
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
l
@expect "z 6"
:load
@expect "0 times nothing present"
@expect "found again from where it was 0 times"
@expect "carried presence"
@magnet off
@run 6000

# A static field at the MMC that nothing confirms - the bench's probe lying
# beside the board (0.02 mT at the MMC, the TMAGs at their zeros) - is a far
# fix until its clock runs out, then absorbed in ONE go: the track ends once,
# and is not found again in bursts (steadiness is judged on the reading as
# measured, not on the baseline-removed one the absorb itself moves: on the
# bench the far track was found again 249 times in twelve minutes, 2026-09-22).
@bias 8 0.006 0.005 -0.018
@run 3000
l
@expect "magnet at"
@run 40000
l
@expect "no magnet"
:load
@expect "found again from where it was 0 times"
@bias 8 0 0 0
z
@run 3000

# Another, weaker magnet - half the strength held at boot: learned from the
# first near fixes (the readings pin it when five sensors read it plainly)
# and the row calibration's taps then take, within a millimetre. (Held at
# 4232 the same magnet fitted 5 mm too high and 20-40 mm off along the
# board, "valid", and no tap ever took.)
@strength 2100
@magnet row 8 3 30 0
@run 1500
c
@run 300
@magnet row 15 1
@run 7000
@expect "held from now on"
@expect "got it: x 26."
@magnet row 15 5
@run 7000
@expect "got it: x 26."
@magnet row 30 1
@run 7000
@expect "got it:"
@expect "z 17."
@magnet row 30 5
@run 7000
@expect "got it:"
@expect "z 17."
c
@run 300
l
@expect "strength: held at"
@magnet off
@run 2000

# The MMC56x3 switched out (the board's default since 2026-09-22 late: "the
# readings have been 100x sketchier since we added that one"): the eight
# TMAG5273s alone track a probe on the board and 40 mm up; 90 mm up is out
# of reach; the status marks the MMC "off".
:mmc off
@expect "ignored by the locator"
@magnet row 14 3
@run 1500
:screen
@expect "row: 14 hole 3"
l
@expect "magnet at"
@magnet 24 22 40 0 0 -1
@run 3000
l
@expect "magnet at"
@magnet 24 22 90 0 0 -1
@run 3000
l
@expect "no magnet"
m
@expect "off"
@magnet off
@run 6000
# ...and a lone TMAG5273 with a stale zero, the MMC out, is no magnet: no
# presence, no lattice every 400 ms (the one-sensor shortcut is the MMC's).
@bias 4 0.045 0 0
@run 3000
l
@expect "no magnet"
@bias 4 0 0 0
@run 1000
