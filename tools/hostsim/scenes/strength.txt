# The magnet's strength learned, and a missing sensor's last reading carrying
# presence (2026-09-22, evening). `make check` runs this after check.txt.
# The regression runs with the MMC56x3 in (the board boots with it out: MAG_USE_MMC_AT_BOOT),
# and with the 2026-09-18 probe's magnet (4232) the scenes were written with, told to the
# locator as a known magnet (the firmware's default is the bench's current one).
:mmc on
:strength 4232
# The settings' record seals a strength as MEASURED only once the value it
# holds is the measured one: for the minute the record still holds the boot
# default it says so (2026-09-23: the seal was written a minute early, and a
# reset inside it restored the default as measured).
@run 3000
s
@expect "strength=1839 unconfirmed"

@seed 7

# A far probe, seen plainly by the MMC56x3 alone: the MMC missing a frame now
# and then (its hand-wired bus does, about once a second on the bench) must
# not end the track - before, every missed frame ended it "nothing present"
# and the next frame found it again (1030 times in 24 minutes: a flicker).
@magnet 24 22 65 0 0 -1
@run 3000
l
@expect "z 6"
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
@dead 8 12
@run 400
l
@expect "z 6"
:load
@expect "them: 0 times nothing present"
@expect "carried presence"
@magnet off
@run 60000

# A static field at the MMC that nothing confirms - the bench's probe lying
# beside the board (0.02 mT at the MMC, the TMAGs at their zeros) - is NOT a
# fix (2026-09-23, Phase 3 step 4: the residuals over every sensor's own
# error, chi 3.7 against 2.0 - the TMAGs read none of what a probe there
# would give them - once their zeros have been followed for a minute with
# nothing there and are doubted at the floor; until then it was a far fix
# for 40 s, then absorbed), and
# the MMC's zero follows it after the hold (keepZeros, MAG_OFFSET_HOLD_S):
# no magnet, once, and not found again in bursts (the bench: 249 times in
# twelve minutes, 2026-09-22).
@bias 8 0.006 0.005 -0.018
:load
@run 3000
l
@expect "only 1 sensor"
@expect "chi 3."
@run 160000
l
@expect "no magnet"
:load
@expect "presence toggled 4 times"
:load
@bias 8 0 0 0
z
@run 3000
