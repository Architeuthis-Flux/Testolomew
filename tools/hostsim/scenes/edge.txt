# The probe off the end of the board with the MMC56x3 out (2026-09-23), at
# two places. First 22 mm past row 1 and 22 mm up: one TMAG5273 reads it
# plainly and four faintly, and the fit lands in a wrong basin (x -36, z 2 for a probe at x -22,
# z 22); with Z weighed by its quietness that basin misfits at 49 % and is
# REJECTED (at 26 % it was accepted as a rough fix); since Phase 3 step 4
# (2026-09-23) a rejected fit is nothing - no rough offer, no far glow - and
# at the second place, 6 mm further out, one TMAG reads 0.033 mT, right at
# the presence hysteresis's lower bar (three quarters of 0.04): present or
# not is a coin flip there and not asserted; nothing is offered either way.
# The lattice's memoryless offer is gone with the rough class.
@reference
:strength 1839
:mmc off
@strength 1839
@seed 3
# (a second for the boot's zero to be judged before anything appears: a
# probe arriving inside the first 64 frames is what the adoption would take
# for the zero, 2026-09-24)
@run 1000
@magnet -22 22 22 0 0 1
@run 3000
l
@expect "no usable fix"
@magnet -28 22 22 0 0 1
@run 3000
l
@expect "track: nothing"
@magnet off
@run 2000

# Two TMAG5273s with stale zeros after a boot (0.045 and 0.03 mT: the
# post-boot range, knobs.md (7)-(8)) and nothing present: NOT a magnet.
# With the seen level scaled for the weak magnet (0.022), two sensors above
# it are not enough to say a probe is there - the corroboration takes two
# TMAGs reading PLAINLY (0.04). With no fix lately the offset filters follow
# the stale zeros out from the first frame (the drift), and presence never
# turns on in the half minute: the count stays at the probe's two toggles
# above (the clocks: two here, twelve at the scaled bar, 2026-09-23).
@bias 2 0.045 0 0
@bias 3 0.03 0 0
:load
@run 30000
:load
@expect "presence toggled 2 times"
l
@expect "no magnet"
@bias 2 0 0 0
@bias 3 0 0 0
@run 2000

# One TMAG5273 reading 60 % high (the 09-21 leave-one-out found TMAG 5 at
# 64 % off the others' prediction) with the probe in row 15: still a fix in
# its hole. The solver spreads that sensor's error over the others, and the
# residuals over every sensor's own error come to a chi of 1.4 against the
# limit of 2.0 - a fix the zero audit gets to work from. (A worse sensor,
# or a bench whose errors the simulator does not have, loosens the limit
# from the tracking page: "fit chi".)
@gain 5 1.6
@magnet row 15 3
@run 3000
l
@expect "magnet at"
@expect "chi 1."
:screen
@expect "row: 15 hole 3"
@magnet off
@gain 5 1.0
@run 2000

# One TMAG5273's zero gone 0.3 mT wrong under a resting probe (a corrupt
# read that sticks - the bus has no CRC - or a magnet left beside the
# sensor): the chi refuses the fit (6 against 2.0 with the zeros doubted at
# the floor, misfit 48 %) on eight plain sensors. A reading three or more TMAGs read plainly is not drift
# (drift is one sensor's): the zeros are held the far hold (2026-09-24;
# followed at the half-minute hold, MAG_OFFSET_HOLD_S, they carried the
# probe's field a minute later and the array was blind to it - observed with
# the rule off), and the fix is back the moment the sensor reads right.
# Held two minutes, then followed: a sensor wrong longer than that under a
# resting probe takes the zeros with it, and the tracking page's "fit chi"
# is the bench's way out. (Two minutes of nothing first: the stale zeros of
# the block above, half followed when its probe arrived, go out, and no fix
# is within the far hold - a reading that was a fix lately keeps the far
# hold anyway; the wrong zero and the probe arrive together.)
@run 125000
@bias 5 0.3 0.3 0.3
@magnet row 15 3
@run 60000
l
@expect "no usable fix"
@expect "chi 6."
@baselines
@seen "baselines over 0.02 mT: none"
@bias 5 0 0 0
# (a cold start: after four rejected in a row the next waits two seconds)
@run 3000
l
@expect "magnet at"
:screen
@expect "row: 15 hole 3"
@magnet off
@run 2000
