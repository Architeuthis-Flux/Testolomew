# The probe off the end of the board with the MMC56x3 out (2026-09-23), at
# two places. First 22 mm past row 1 and 22 mm up: one TMAG5273 reads it
# plainly and four faintly, and the fit lands in a wrong basin (x -36, z 2 for a probe at x -22,
# z 22); with Z weighed by its quietness that basin misfits at 49 % and is
# REJECTED (at 26 % it was accepted as a rough fix); since Phase 3 step 4
# (2026-09-23) a rejected fit is nothing - no rough offer, no far glow - and
# at the second place, 6 mm further out, one TMAG reads 0.038 mT and the
# array says no magnet (presence takes two reading plainly). The lattice's
# memoryless offer is gone with the rough class.
:mmc off
@strength 1839
@seed 3
@magnet -22 22 22 0 0 1
@run 3000
l
@expect "no usable fix"
@magnet -28 22 22 0 0 1
@run 3000
l
@expect "no magnet"
@expect "track: nothing"
@magnet off
@run 2000

# Two TMAG5273s with stale zeros after a boot (0.045 and 0.03 mT: the
# post-boot range, knobs.md (7)-(8)) and nothing present: NOT a magnet.
# With the seen level scaled for the weak magnet (0.022), two sensors above
# it are not enough to say a probe is there - the corroboration takes two
# TMAGs reading PLAINLY (0.04). The offset filters follow the stale zeros out
# at once (nothing present, no magnet lately): presence never toggles in the
# half minute - the count stays at the two toggles of the probe above (the
# clocks let the 0.045 zero plus noise put two sensors over 0.04 now and then:
# two toggles here, twelve at the scaled bar; 2026-09-23).
@bias 2 0.045 0 0
@bias 3 0.03 0 0
:load
@run 30000
:load
@expect "presence toggled 2 times"
l
@expect "no magnet"
@bias 2 0 0 0
@bias 3 0 0 0
@run 2000

# One TMAG5273 reading 60 % high (the 09-21 leave-one-out found TMAG 5 at
# 64 % off the others' prediction) with the probe in row 15: still a fix in
# its hole. The solver spreads that sensor's error over the others, and the
# residuals over every sensor's own error come to a chi of 1.4 against the
# limit of 2.0 - a fix the zero audit gets to work from. (A worse sensor,
# or a bench whose errors the simulator does not have, loosens the limit
# from the tracker page: "fit chi".)
@gain 5 1.6
@magnet row 15 3
@run 3000
l
@expect "magnet at"
@expect "chi 1."
:screen
@expect "row: 15 hole 3"
@magnet off
@gain 5 1.0
@run 2000
