# Long hovers (2026-09-23): the offset filters that replace the absorb clocks
# (Phase 3 step 3) must not eat a probe held still at the edge of reach.
# (i) The MMC56x3's far regime: 90 mm up for 90 s stays a fix, with no cold
# start after the first (the clocks absorbed a steady one after 120 s; a
# filter that absorbed it at all ended it in 45 s: the MMC's presence margin
# at 90 mm is 20 %). (ii) The TMAG5273s alone: a probe off the end of the
# board for a minute. Its readings fit a WRONG basin (x -36, z 1 for a probe
# at x -22, z 22) within every sensor's error, and the footprint rule
# (MAGLOC_OUTSIDE_MM: beyond the array, seen plainly by too few) refuses it.
# A reading refused only by the footprint is a dipole of the held strength
# the array cannot pin, and is held the far hold (MAG_OFFSET_FAR_HOLD_S)
# like a one-sensor fix, not followed as drift at the half-minute hold
# (MAG_OFFSET_HOLD_S, 2026-09-24: followed after 30 s, the return over row
# 15 was refused at the chi's limit on the absorbed 0.04 mT and the probe on
# the board was then followed too). After a minute: still no usable fix,
# nothing offered, the zeros untouched; back over row 15 the probe is a fix
# in its hole.
