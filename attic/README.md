# Attic

Things that were built, tried on the bench and dropped. Not in the build, not tested; kept because the project has no history yet.

- `two-magnet/`: `MagProbeFit` (a rigid two-magnet probe fitted as one thing: tip position, shaft direction, roll, and a learnable shape - spacing, each magnet's angle to the shaft, the back magnet's turn round it) and its Unity tests. Dropped on 2026-09-17 after two bench sessions: on this array the fields such a probe makes are 0.1-0.3 mT against the array's own ~0.05 mT of unexplained error, so no shape fitted better than 5-8 % and the tip came out no better than one magnet's. The full account is in `docs/wireless-probe-sensing.md`. Orientation now comes from the one magnet's pole (magnetised along the shaft), `MAGLOC_TIP_OFFSET_MM`.
- `far-field-gradient/`: `MagFar`, a closed-form "roughly where is the magnet" from the field and its gradient tensor at the array's centre (Nara, Suzuki and Ando 2006: `G r = -3 B`, with the out-of-plane gradients from Maxwell), meant for a probe held far away. The sign convention checks out (a magnet 150 mm up is found within 4 mm from perfect readings), but on this array the gradient is a finite difference across 54 x 44 mm, and at every distance where there is signal that is not a derivative: `farscan.cpp` gives 25-50 % of the distance in error at 50-70 mm with NO noise, 10 % only beyond 90 mm where a probe magnet reads 0.006 mT. Dropped 2026-09-17 in favour of a coarse lattice search inside `MagFit` (`magFitCoarse`), which never iterates either and lands within one step of the answer at any distance. Would work on a small, dense gradiometer, not on a breadboard-sized array.
